# Review of the Witness Angel Recorder specification

Scope: `README.md`, `01-architecture-and-platform.md`, `02-ux-and-screens.md`, `03-business-logic.md`, `04-data-and-protocols.md`, `05-open-issues.md` and `fixtures/` (Recorder 1.0).

Method:

- Full read of the six files.
- Spot checks against the sources at the README baselines:
  - `REC` `main` @ `bbf5d17`;
  - `COMP` and `CRYPTO` exports of `warecorder_stable` (`68bdcd6` and `b91aaf4`; both commits confirmed with `git rev-parse` in the workspace clones);
  - the Authenticator's cryptolib `poc/pure-python-crypto-utils` @ `2717a79`, used by `waflet_components`;
  - `waflet_components` itself.
- Tools run:
  - the fixture self-check, on a scratch copy;
  - an independent recomputation, with pycryptodome alone, of the symmetric test vectors, the key-wrapping sizes and the OSC datagrams;
  - a link and anchor checker;
  - a script that checks every `PREFIX/path:line` citation against the file length.

## Overall verdict

**Excellent groundwork, but one blocker before a Flet port starts.** The spec reuses what worked in the Authenticator spec after its review, and does it better:

- normative tags and one owner per topic;
- one open-issue list (99 items) with dispositions;
- a normative interop checklist (04 §0);
- an exact key-wrapping pipeline with real byte sizes;
- self-checking fixtures produced by the real cryptolib.

Accuracy is high:

- Every byte-level claim I recomputed is right.
- Every settings default and every state-machine transition I traced is right.
- Every bug I re-checked (OI-08, OI-49, OI-66, OI-75, OI-84) is real.
- 529 of 539 line citations are in range, and the link checker finds no broken link.

The problems are not in the 1.0 description but in the bridge to the planned target:

1. **The cryptolib for the reimplementation is unspecified, and the obvious choice breaks interop.** The spec says crypto MUST go through `wacryptolib` and that `waflet_components` should be reused. That package runs on the Authenticator's cryptolib branch. On that branch, decrypting an existing recorder cryptainer raises `KeyError: 'payload_ciphertext_signatures'` (I reproduced it on the fixtures). It also writes cryptainers that `warecorder_stable` cannot read. 04 §4.5 says the opposite ("converts a legacy `payload_signatures` on read").
2. **Several *fix* dispositions need a protocol the spec does not define.** Examples: reporting why a start was refused, per-sensor health, storage full. The spec also never checks that the target GUI toolkit runs on the Raspberry Pi hardware it must support.
3. A handful of small inaccuracies, mislinked issue IDs and over-strict MUSTs.

Counts: **1 blocker, 4 major, 8 minor, 4 nit.**

Severity scale:

- **blocker**: cannot implement interoperably without a decision or a guess.
- **major**: likely wrong implementation or significant wasted effort.
- **minor**: local error or polish that a careful reader would work around.
- **nit**: cosmetic.
